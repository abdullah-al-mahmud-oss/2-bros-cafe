About this website

This is a static informational website for 2 Bros Cafe. It has no account system, contact form, shopping cart, or first-party analytics configured. The site does not intentionally collect information through its own forms or scripts.

Hosting and technical logs

The hosting provider may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser details, and requested files in server logs to operate and secure the website. The hosting provider controls its own retention and privacy practices; check the provider's policy for details.

External services and links

Links to Google Maps, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, telephone, and email open services outside this website. Those providers may receive information when you follow a link or use their services, under their own privacy policies. This site does not embed a third-party map or load third-party analytics, advertising, or remote fonts.

Cookies and analytics

No analytics or advertising scripts are configured on this website. The site itself does not set cookies. External services may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies after you visit them.
